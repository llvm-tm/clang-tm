APPENDIX H — Common Rust Mistakes in Transactional Memory
Lines: ~203 | Sections: 6 (\section*, unnumbered) | Listings: 5

PURPOSE: Field guide of the five recurring Rust-vs-TM mistakes, each with a
wrong-then-right listing.

SECTIONS (all \section*)
- Mistake 1: Forgetting the Abort Signal
    Treating a contention abort as a returned Result instead of an unwind;
    the transaction closure must be panic-through-safe.
- Mistake 2: Closure Captures and Lifetime Errors
    Capturing &mut across the retry boundary; what to capture instead.
- Mistake 3: Send and Sync Violations
    Moving TxHandle/!Sync state across threads; why transaction() bounds the
    handle to one thread.
- Mistake 4: Incorrect Memory Ordering
    Hand-rolled atomics around TmCell; which fences the runtime already owns.
- Mistake 5: Mutable Aliasing Through Unsafe
    Aliasing the UnsafeCell interior outside a transaction; breaks the
    opaque-promise and the borrow invariant both.
- Summary
