PURPOSE: Math toolkit for TM reasoning: automata/languages, Markov chains,
queueing theory (Little's law), formal histories and correctness/progress
conditions, plus worked examples applying them to the bank transfer.

SECTIONS
- Automata and Formal Languages
    DFAs, regular languages, recognizers for traces.
- Markov Chains
    Discrete-time Markov chains, absorbing states, expected attempts.
- Queueing Theory and Little's Law
    L = lambda * S; expected attempts = 1/(1-p) (geometric).
- Histories, Legality, and Opacity
    History/event vocabulary; committed projection; real-time order;
    serialization and legality; serializability vs strict serializability
    vs opacity; VWC first mention; TMS1/TMS2 provenance; model-checker
    tractability of opacity.
- Progress and the Family of Correctness Conditions
  * The Progress Ladder and the Fall of Obstruction-Freedom
    Wait-free > lock-free > obstruction-free; progressiveness (weak/strong)
    and conflict-freedom; Kuznetsov-Ravi lower bounds for OF TMs and the
    linear blocking-vs-nonblocking separation; companion backends are
    blocking by design; tab:progress-taxonomy maps every backend family
    to its progress guarantee.
  * Safety Beyond Opacity: VWC and the TMS Specifications
    TMS1 (one execution for commits) vs TMS2 (one memory-state sequence);
    Doherty et al. formal I/O-automaton specs; VWC (one world per causal
    past); machine-checked chain TMS2 => opacity => TMS1 (Lesani et al.);
    fig:condition-lattice; which condition a backend should target
    (pure transactions vs leaking compensating actions).
- Worked Example: Numbers, Not Just Symbols
    Concrete numbers for the bank retry math.
- Worked Example: Simulating Retry Cost in the Bank
    Simulation of abort/retry cost.
- Worked Example: Model Checking Money Conservation
    TLA+ money-conservation model.
- Worked Example: Recognizing Transaction Trace Records
    DFA that recognizes transaction trace records.
- Worked Example: Two-Phase Validation Cost
    Cost analysis of 2-phase validation.

- Exercises
